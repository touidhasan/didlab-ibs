# Bitget, September 2026

Unauthorized transfers drained part of Bitget's hot and warm wallet tiers (three-tier architecture; cold wallets
unaffected), five weeks before the Binance incident and in the same class.

## Facts and sources

- Detection time, three-tier architecture, initial $351.6M: Bitget security notice (CEO Gracy Chen); Forklog.
- Revision to $387.5M; custodial exchange wallets only: The Block; Fieldfisher.
- Mechanism (backend system breached, transaction data spoofed, exchange's authorization process triggered) and attribution: BleepingComputer (CEO statement).

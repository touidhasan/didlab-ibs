# UPCX, April 2025

An attacker with access to a privileged UPCX address upgraded the ProxyAdmin contract and executed withdrawByAdmin,
moving 18.4M UPC (about $70M) from three management accounts.

## Facts and sources

- Mechanism and amount: Cyvers via Cointelegraph and The Defiant; Halborn explainer (likely compromised key).

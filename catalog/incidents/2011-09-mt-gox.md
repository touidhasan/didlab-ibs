# Mt. Gox, 2011-2014

According to the DOJ, from about September 2011 through at least May 2014 the attackers used access to the server
holding Mt. Gox's wallets and private keys to move about 647,000 BTC to addresses they controlled. Mt. Gox halted
in February 2014. The date field is approximate (DOJ: "around September 2011"); the dataset dates the incident to
the 2014 collapse.

## Facts and sources

- Access path, period, amount (647,000 BTC): DOJ indictments unsealed June 2023, via The Record, CoinDesk and Duo Decipher.
- USD figure: DeFiLlama snapshot of 2026-10-31. Press figures from 2023 value the coins at 2023 prices and are not used.

# LuBian, December 2020

About 127,426 BTC ($3.5B at the time) left wallets associated with the LuBian mining pool on December 28-29, 2020.
The pool's keys appear to have come from a weak generator. Whether this was a theft by an outsider is contested
since the October 2025 forfeiture action against the Prince Group.

## Facts and sources

- Amount, date, weak-key explanation: Arkham via The Block and heise; Elliptic.
- DOJ forfeiture and the link to the Prince Group: Elliptic; Cointelegraph.
- Competing interpretations: CryptoSlate; Bitcoin.com.

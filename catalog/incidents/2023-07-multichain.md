# Multichain, July 2023

About $126M left Multichain's MPC-controlled bridge addresses after its CEO, who alone controlled the MPC
servers, had been detained.

## Facts and sources

- CEO detention, MPC servers under his account, Kunming IP: Multichain statement via CoinDesk, Blockworks and IBTimes.
- Amount and Chainalysis's view: Risky Biz News; CoinJournal.

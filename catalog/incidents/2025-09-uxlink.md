# UXLINK, September 2025

An address executed a delegateCall on UXLINK's multisig, removed the admin role, added a new owner with
addOwnerWithThreshold, then moved stablecoins, WBTC and ETH and minted UXLINK tokens.

## Facts and sources

- Mechanism and initial $11.3M: The Block (Cyvers); BeInCrypto.
- Loss estimates differ: Cointelegraph (Cyvers vs Hacken); Rekt (about $41M total operation).
- Private key leak reported: ChainCatcher.

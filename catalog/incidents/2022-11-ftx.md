# FTX, November 2022

According to the indictment, co-conspirators SIM-swapped an employee of "Victim Company-1", reported to be FTX,
and moved over $400M from its wallets on November 11-12, 2022.

## Facts and sources

- Indictment details (SIM swap, employee, over $400M, dates): CoinDesk; CNBC; Cointelegraph.
- Victim identification: Bloomberg via CoinDesk and Cointelegraph; Elliptic via Cointelegraph.

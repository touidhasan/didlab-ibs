# WazirX, July 2024

A Safe multisig wallet held by WazirX (six signers: five WazirX, one Liminal; three WazirX approvals plus Liminal's
final approval were required) was drained of about $230-235M. The transaction shown to signers differed from the
payload that was executed.

## Facts and sources

- Signer structure and loss: PYMNTS; Inc42; CoinDesk.
- WazirX's account (interface discrepancy) and Liminal's account (three compromised WazirX devices; wallet created outside Liminal's ecosystem): Cointelegraph; CoinDesk; TechCrunch.
- Third-party review for Liminal (Grant Thornton): Inc42.
- Attribution to North Korea-linked actors: Elliptic, via Decrypt.

# Ronin bridge, March 2022

173,600 ETH and 25.5M USDC were withdrawn from the Ronin bridge in two transactions using five of nine validator keys
(four Sky Mavis, one Axie DAO). The theft went unnoticed for six days.

## Facts and sources

- Mechanism, amounts, 5-of-9 keys, discovery on March 29: Ronin post-mortem; Security Affairs (quoting Sky Mavis); Halborn.
- Attack path through the gas-free RPC node / Axie DAO allowlist: SlowMist analysis; Ronin post-mortem.
- Attribution: FBI (Lazarus), as stated in the Ronin post-mortem.

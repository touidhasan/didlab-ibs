# Nomad, August 2022

A faulty upgrade made Nomad's Replica contract accept unproven messages, and about $190M was drained in hours by
the first attacker and many copycats.

## Facts and sources

- Mechanism: Immunefi; samczsun via Decrypt; CertiK.
- Amount (at least $190.7M): PeckShield via GovInfoSecurity.

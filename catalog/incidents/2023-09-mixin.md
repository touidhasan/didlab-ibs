# Mixin Network, September 2023

About $200M was drained from Mixin Network's hot wallets after the database of its cloud service provider was
breached.

## Facts and sources

- Date, cause (cloud provider database), amount: Mixin via Cointelegraph and SecurityWeek; Halborn.
- Traced amount: PeckShield via Cointelegraph.
